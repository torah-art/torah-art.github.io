The Bible and the Artist

The Artist and the Church: Reflections

2014 · The Bible and the Artist

A creative person does not always feel at home in today's church. The church's attitude to people of the arts is mixed. In my view this subject is becoming more and more pressing with time. And it is an international phenomenon.

As for Israel, you can safely multiply any problem connected with creativity by ten.

The joke about a Jew coming down the airplane steps in Tel Aviv is no longer a joke today: if you see a Jew coming down the steps at Israel's international airport, Ben Gurion, and he is not holding a violin, it means he plays the piano… The number of artists per head of population in Israel goes beyond all "reasonable" limits. Really, you cannot expect every city neighbourhood to have its own symphony orchestra. And how many paintings can the average Israeli actually buy? They are not radishes, bought by the bunch. And they, that is, we Jews, keep on coming. And each one has a paintbrush in his hands, or a violin, or a clarinet, or…

So the pressure on the average congregation of believers grows too. Don't you think the time has come to deal with this question?
