The Musician. Yuval, Son of Lemekh: the Audiobook

2024 · My Art Projects, The Bible and the Artist

The full audio version of the book "The Musician. Yuval, Son of Lemekh", written by Ephraim Kalish and read by Vadim Birman. It is a reflection on the first musician mentioned in the Bible (Gen. 4:21), on the spiritual nature of art and on the path of inspiration. Listen online or download all 7 parts free of charge. The recording uses music by Gevorg Khechoyan and Boris Rysin.
