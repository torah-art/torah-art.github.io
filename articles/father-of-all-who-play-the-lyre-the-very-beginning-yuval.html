Father of All Who Play the Lyre. The Very Beginning: Yuval

2014 · Art Midrash

The Torah calls him the father of all musicians. He is the very first person whom Holy Scripture calls a musician, and one who left a school behind him.

The very beginning

A conversation with the excellent musician Timur Khusnutdinov from Izhevsk.

Video in Russian

There is a well-established tradition of seeing Yuval and his brothers as an accursed generation bearing the mark of Cain, so that nothing good could come of their deeds and actions. Their life was dreary and accursed…?

Well, every point of view has a right to exist. It is only important to note that this is not the only possible way of understanding and interpreting the text of the fourth chapter about the last generation of the line of Cain.

Moreover, the Torah calls Yuval the father of all musicians. He is the very first person whom Holy Scripture calls a musician, and one who left a school behind him: "And his brother's name was Yuval; he was the father of all who play the lyre and the pipe" (Gen. 4:21).

A hint lies in the names of the brothers themselves, if we look at them in their original Hebrew sound. If we pay attention, we will see many hints of God's intervention and God's hand in the destiny of the children of the accursed one… More on this in one of the next midrashim.
