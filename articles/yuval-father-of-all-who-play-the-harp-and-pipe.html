Yuval, Father of All Who Play the Harp and Pipe

2019 · The Bible and the Artist

The father of all who play the harp and the pipe. That is what is said of the first musician mentioned in the Bible.

Watch the video on Facebook (video in Russian).

"19 And Lamech took unto him two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah. 20 And Adah bare Jabal: he was the father of such as dwell in tents, and of such as have cattle. 21 And his brother's name was Jubal: he was the father of all such as handle the harp and organ. 22 And Zillah, she also bare Tubalcain, an instructer of every artificer in brass and iron: and the sister of Tubalcain was Naamah." (Gen. 4:19–22)
