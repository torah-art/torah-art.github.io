Art Midrash

Awake, North Wind! (Song of Songs 4:16)

2018 · Art Midrash, The Bible and the Artist

The wind from the north is an image of the Holy Spirit, a wind that brings life-giving moisture and coolness. In Hebrew, as is well known, wind and spirit are one and the same word.

Watch the video on Facebook (video in Russian).

This part of our art midrash was devoted to the garden, PARDES (the ways of interpreting the Bible in Jewish tradition).

"12 A garden inclosed is my sister, my spouse; a spring shut up, a fountain sealed. 13 Thy plants are an orchard of pomegranates, with pleasant fruits; camphire, with spikenard, 14 Spikenard and saffron; calamus and cinnamon, with all trees of frankincense; myrrh and aloes, with all the chief spices: 15 A fountain of gardens, a well of living waters, and streams from Lebanon.
16 Awake, O north wind; and come, thou south; blow upon my garden, that the spices thereof may flow out. Let my beloved come into his garden, and eat his pleasant fruits." (Song 4:12–16)
