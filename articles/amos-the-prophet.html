Amos the Prophet

2014 · Galleries

Amos the Prophet. Shepherd Listening to God

The most popular image of the Bible is a shepherd. For us as believers shepherd means a man who has an intent eye, and what is the most important the one who is able to hear and to listen. The same way as God had called an ordinary shepherd to serve Him, today He calls to serve Him everyone who is ready to listen and obey His will.

The year 2957 according to the Jewish calendar.
