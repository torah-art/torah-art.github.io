Haifa Celebrates Purim 2015

2015 · Galleries, Biblical Feasts

There is a special festive atmosphere in Ir ha-Tachtit (the Lower City) of Haifa. Haifa is celebrating PURIM! Joy, kind smiles. Lots of people. Everyone, young and old, is filled with the importance and solemnity of this moment of be-yachad (being together) and rejoicing together.

Photos by Veta Kalish.
