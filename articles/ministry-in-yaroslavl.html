Art Midrash

Ministry in Yaroslavl

2014 · Art Midrash, Biblical Feasts

In Yaroslavl we were in the church where Andrew Direnko is pastor. It was very special to meet there with actors of the Christian theatre "Ecclesiastes". All these people have such a fire for God! They understand their great responsibility before Him for that gift of creativity He had given to them. They also have a desire to inflame with creativity every born again believer.

Now we pray and ask God to show us the perspectives of the possible co-work with our Russian brothers and sisters. I believe that our Father loves when His children can agree with each other and do something together. I also believe that He had prepared for this His special power and anointing.

This opportunity to serve together as Israeli and Russian believers, to serve churches and to serve artists in Russia and in Israel, seems to be very important.

As you probably know, the main commandment for this feast is to blow shofar and to listen to the cry of shofar.(Numbers 29:1).
So I spoke about the mystery of blowing shofar:
Ps. 89:15 – in the original Hebrew -
“Blessed the nation, who knows the sound of blowing shofar”
Amos 3:6-8 “Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people not be afraid?”

In the beginning before each part I prayed a very short prayer and then I blew the shofar.

Shofar - Yovel

Ps. 139:2–3 (prayer)

Isa. 61:1–3
