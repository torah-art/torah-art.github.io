Biblical Feasts

Obeying the Commandment of Shofar Blowing

2015 · Biblical Feasts

Creativity and tradition

The Bible tells us little about how exactly Israel should blow the shofar. I study the Jewish tradition and the commentaries of our sages. My goal is to keep everything valuable that clearly agrees with the text of the Bible and reveals its symbolic meaning.

I believe that we need to respect our fathers' traditions and their experience in obeying God's commandments. It is important to look at the whole of that experience in a creative way, seeking the will of God at the same time, and to renew the traditional way of blowing the shofar.

Fragments of Rosh Hashanah shofar blowing

Here are a few episodes from congregational meetings and celebrations of the holy feasts, where I blow the shofar, obeying the Lord's commandment to Israel.

One of the names of the shofar in the Bible is Yovel (Jubilee). This video shows a fragment of Rosh Hashanah shofar blowing (the Feast of Trumpets). It is a musical interpretation of the name Yovel (Jubilee).
