The Bible and the Artist

Asking Questions. Artist Oleksandr Antonyuk

2018 · The Bible and the Artist

For me the Bible is an artistic thing. A person strives towards where he feels good and comfortable. For me the Bible is a comfortable place where I rest, recover, receive new thoughts and directions, where I am built up, where my renewal is helped. You can call it different things; I simply feel comfortable and good there.

Video in Russian

In principle, a person looks for pleasure. Pleasure is the fulfilment of a need. If a person gets his needs satisfied, he gets pleasure. If there is no need, there can be no result in the form of this pleasure; let us call it happiness. And so, if a person has no hunger to begin with in the spiritual sphere or, for example, in art, if a person feels no need, it is so hard to drag him into a museum or get him to read a book. He gets no pleasure from it, because he has no need of it.

So if there is an inner need, a person may put himself on some artificial substitute, satisfying this need of his in another way.

When I read and studied the Scriptures closely, this began to form in me a certain inner position, based on the Bible itself and not on an abstract idea of it...
