Israel and the Church

Interview with Jacob Damkani about the Film "A New Spirit". On Messianic Jews

2018 · Israel and the Church

For the first time in the history of Israel, a full-length feature film about Messianic believers, "A New Spirit", has come out in cinemas. It is based on Jacob Damkani's book "Why Me?" and tells of the conversion of a Sephardi Jew from the north of Israel who grew up in a poor neighbourhood and sank to "the bottom of life".

Just think: this is the world's first Israeli Messianic full-length feature film!! It's a bombshell! As Jacob Damkani joked: "Since the destruction of the Second Temple, this film is a historic event of its kind!"

Video in Russian

The film was shot in two languages (English and Hebrew).

Jacob Damkani (leader of the ministry Trumpet of Salvation to Israel, author of the book "Why Me?") was interviewed by Mark Abramov (leader of a Messianic congregation in Tel Aviv, Toraart special correspondent).

Jacob Damkani (footage from the documentary): We pray day and night that Israel may receive the true answer!

A Hasid (footage from the documentary): This is a sect of missionaries. They are trying to lead you astray; guard your souls! Missionaries, get out of here!

Doron Eran (film director): In Israel there are about 20,000 people who belong to the Messianic community. One of them is Jacob Damkani. And this is his personal story.

The head of the Israel Journalists' Association: This is an important subject, one that opens eyes and awakens thought. A great deal more will be said about it!

M.A. (leader of a Messianic congregation in Tel Aviv, Toraart special correspondent): How did it happen, and whose idea was it to make a full-length feature film about Messianic Jews? After all, among other things, it takes a lot of money.

An Israeli TV presenter: The Israeli film "A New Spirit", directed by Doron Eran. The film's budget is more than 2 million dollars.

J.D. (author of the book "Why Me?", leader of the ministry Trumpet of Salvation to Israel): It all began with a proposal by a Messianic Jew to make a documentary about Messianic Judaism in Israel. And in this project, which was brought to the director Doron Eran, I was one of those interviewed.

J.D. (footage from the documentary): Yeshua was a Jew; He did not bring a new religion.
A voice off camera: "May your name be blotted out! Get out of our country!"

J.D.: And then the cameraman brought all the footage with interviews of many people from the Messianic movement. And the director was looking at all this footage on his screen. And when he noticed me on the video, one of the many people interviewed, he said: "We need to make a film about this guy!" Evidently he saw my heart! He saw my thirst for God, for the truth. And so the documentary "The Messenger" (Ha-Mevaser) was made.

I realised I was dealing with a sincere, upright man with a right heart, who is interested in the development of Israeli society. And he wanted to show the people the idea of the Messianic movement.

The TV presenter: Is he a Jew, or is he a Christian?
Doron Eran: He is absolutely and completely a Jew, a Judean! The Messianic Jewish congregations are a Jewish community.

J.D.: And so we decided to take it on and make a feature film.

M.A.: We are talking about the director Doron Eran?

Doron Eran: And do you know what? He is a deeply believing Jew! And everything they'll babble to you, "Oh, these converts! Oh, missionaries!!", it's all nonsense!

J.D.: Yes, the director Doron Eran.

M.A.: So how did a well-known director from Tel Aviv, an atheist, take on such a project?

J.D.: The subject itself spoke to him. He was interested in getting to the bottom of it; he cares about Israel. He has devoted his whole life to serving Israeli society. And in the Messianic idea he found an answer for the healing of the human heart.

He realised he was taking a risk. And indeed, now that the film has come out, he is receiving no few curses. These are his words: the first time I visit the association of Israeli film-makers, they'll throw me down the stairs, because everyone now looks at me as a missionary, a convert, an opponent of Judaism... But he knows that none of this is true. Unfortunately our people are still blinded and still think that Yeshua is another religion, that Yeshua is pagan Christianity. And we must do the work so that our people realise that the true Judaism of the Tanakh is precisely in Yeshua. He is the natural continuation of the Tanakh.

Doron Eran understood this; he lived through it. And now he is paying a high price for it. He tells me he is beginning to fear for the continuation of his career. We write to each other a lot, meet, talk. And I told him: free your heart from this worry. The hand of God is on you, and He will take care of you and your needs!

M.A.: But how did he make up his mind? After all, he knew he was taking a big risk. And the screenwriter and the whole film crew understood that this was a dangerous, risky venture...

J.D.: Doron, what you have done is truly a historic event since the destruction of the Second Temple!

J.D.: Inner conviction made him do it. He understood that this is the truth. And that it was right to take on this subject. Since his youth he had known that the Judaism of the ultra-Orthodox is not what corresponds to the truth. In his circle people understood that all these "Orthodox ghettos" of Mea Shearim and Bnei Brak have no light in them, and at the same time they impose on the whole people of Israel their idea of "the right way". And he wanted to fight this. He very much wanted to push it out of the way. And when he understood about salvation and deliverance and the forgiveness of sins, when he understood the truth, he understood that he held in his hands a weapon with which to fight the lie that has taken hold of Israelis' minds.

M.A.: The acting is simply wonderful. Even though most of the actors are not believers. But they acted wonderfully. It will be not only in cinemas but also on television?

J.D.: Exactly; we are making five episodes for television.

M.A.: And you watch it and believe these are real people.

J.D.: All the actors playing the main roles, I am sure, read my book, on which the film is based. And I had an amazing opportunity to share the good news with people whose work influences the whole nation. Not only actors, but also sound engineers, musicians, lighting technicians, make-up artists. Imagine: 70 people! Fourteen hours of shooting a day for two months. It was a colossal project. And thank God, He touched them; they learned things they had never heard before.

M.A.: What about the main character, played by the actor Imri Biton? To play you in the film, did he have to get to know you deeply?

Imri Biton (actor): A man who starts from the very bottom and step by step overcomes a great deal by faith...

J.D.: Imri Biton of course read my book and came to my home in Jaffa. We spent a lot of time together. Like me, he grew up in a poor neighbourhood. And at some stage of his life he was a criminal, as I was in my youth. So of all of them he was the most suitable candidate for the role.

Mark Abramov: I have received many letters saying that the film moves people to go out on the streets to share the good news and not to be afraid. So we have reached the goal of opening the eyes of believers in Israel and giving them an example of bold evangelism, so as to fulfil Yeshua's commandment to make disciples. So I am grateful to you for your example.

J.D.: I want to say from all my heart: to those who are born of the Spirit, if we really understand what it means to be born from above, God has given boldness and all power and has freed us from fear.

The point is to look deep inside ourselves, where no one sees or knows: whom do we really enthrone in our heart? Who really is the King, Lord and God of our life? If we have received the good news through understanding who Yeshua is, who came to redeem and cleanse us and write us in the book of life, then our life now belongs to Him, and we really let Him be the King of kings of our whole life and of everything that belongs to us. And it does not matter where and as what we work: as a doctor, a lawyer, a street sweeper, a builder; we are bound to put the Kingdom of the Lord at the head of the corner. And then there is no fear and no worry, and our mind is occupied with how to advance His kingdom, the good news, among the people around us. And the people of Israel need to wake up and see Yeshua as the King of the Jews.

Here is a wonderful book, "Why Me?"; it is also available in Russian, and you can order it free of charge and receive as many copies as you want.

And invite your friends to see the film "A New Spirit", based on the real events described in this book.

(Footage from the film "A New Spirit"):
"And you: either you behave as you should, or you'll be thrown out of this house!"

"Get out of here, you piece of dirt!"

"He's a convert dressed up as a Jew! May your name be blotted out!!"

J.D.: Not everyone is called to stand at crossroads; not everyone has to go and hand out leaflets in the street. But let us not be ashamed of the good news, and let us use every opportunity we have for it. Because only by serving in this way will we learn to be good messengers of the good news.

The author of this book has an unusual destiny. He was born in the land of Israel, in Galilee, and his childhood was spent among the same hills where the Saviour and Messiah of Israel once walked. But only many years later, in America, did Jacob for the first time take in his hands the New Testament, forbidden to an observant Jew, and it was as if he had come home. He was surprised to learn that Yeshua, despised and rejected by his people, is flesh of the flesh and blood of the blood of that people.

This is a gripping story of how God found one of His lost sons. Now Jacob preaches the Gospel on the streets of Israel's cities and dreams of the day when his whole people will find their true Jewish roots, as he found his.
Translated from English. This is a new, revised and expanded edition of the book.
