The Bible and the Artist

Into Our Own Wardrobe: Reflections on Fashion

2014 · The Bible and the Artist

Clothing designer Lesya Repetivskaya. Why, you may ask, an article about fashion on a site devoted to a biblical view of creativity?

No, this is not a fight for ratings or a wish to be fashionable. It is a matter of principle for me. It is important for me to see and hear the creative statements of people in different artistic professions (a cook, a painter, a musician, a poet and so on), their view of creativity in the light of the Holy Scriptures, and what has been revealed to them in creative work as a means of knowing and drawing near to the truth.

Today my guest, Lesya Repetivskaya, a clothing designer, stylist and a refined, elegant woman, shares with readers her thoughts on how a woman can and should be beautiful, regardless of how much she spends, without chasing after more and more new things…

Video filming and editing: Vadim Birman.

"Speaking of how a woman should dress and present herself, I will say this: there should be a gentle attractiveness, and there must be femininity.

When we put on heels and skirts, we feel quite different. We feel softer. When we are in trainers and jeans, we are almost men. And I don't think that is quite what we should be aiming for. In order to have manly men beside us (forgive the tautology), really manly men, we must be feminine women. Feminine… And since we are much more flexible than men, we must begin with ourselves. Clothing should above all be feminine. Besides being sisters and wives, we are mothers, the beloved.

We go NOT TO THE SHOP but INTO OUR OWN WARDROBE

The aim of our project is to go not to the shop but into our own wardrobe, and to mix all those things with current trends. In principle you need not buy anything more.

For those of us brought up in the harsh conditions of Soviet times, the lack of everything that could have taught a woman in this respect formed a wrong idea of how many clothes one should have. Usually things were bought for a holiday, when the chance came, somehow obtained. And this lack of clothes subconsciously formed an unrestrained thirst for acquiring things. That is very frightening, because we have taught all this to our daughters. We passed it on at the genetic level: that there may not be enough clothes.

And when we broke free from school uniforms and went our own way, it was a little ugly. We started with radical mini, then midi, then maxi. Fortunately, to my great joy, by the year 2000 fashion as a term had outlived itself. Now everything is in fashion. The idea of fashion practically does not exist. What remains is the idea of style. And style is much better than fashion, because every woman can find her niche, that unique, inimitable style which will make her vivid, unforgettable, attractive to her man. And he will admire her because she is the only one like her! And she will win respect from the other brothers.

We must pass on the art of being feminine to our daughters. If they do not learn it from us, it will be very hard for them. Today's world promotes the unisex style, that is, making men and women the same. And that runs so much against God's plans for each of us, for men and for women, that my personal enemy is unisex.

I want a different style to take shape among believers

I very much want a completely different style to take shape among believers: one that will not say "here I am, take me", not the style of kitsch, the style of availability. It will be the style of a royal person, without pride or haughtiness, simply with an inner harmony and the knowledge of who you are in God's eyes.

A woman who is well, stylishly and beautifully dressed, without aggressive sexuality, conveys that she is not available. Not every man, and I would even say far from every man, will come up in the street to get acquainted with a woman who is well and modestly dressed, beautiful, carrying dignity in herself. This is quite a strong protection against unwanted intrusions into a woman's territory, which can sometimes be very insulting. For modern fashion seems to declare that a woman is available to all. Such an appearance can stop unwanted admirers.

Forget the rules we were brought up with. Just forget them. Whatever was valuable will come back. You simply need to start with a clean slate, stop thinking of yourselves as Europeans (Lesya now lives in Israel and means here the Middle Eastern and Mediterranean style – editor's note), look around and see this wonderful culture of the East. It is naive. In my youth it seemed primitive to me. But now I understand that there is so much life in the culture of the East, so much divine love, that it is no shame to push one's European culture somewhere into the shade. Alas, it has not justified itself. Look inside, let go of childhood dreams, try everything that once seemed beyond you."

In the video you will also find a whole series of practical tips from a top-class stylist and comments on various compositional solutions in clothing. It is worth watching and listening… not to mention Lesya's gift as a storyteller, her intonation, grace and light humour.
