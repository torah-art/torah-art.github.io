Biblical Feasts

The Feast. Creating Through Memory

2018 · Biblical Feasts

One of the names for a man in biblical Hebrew is ZAKHAR, from the verb "to remember". That is, a human being is one who keeps a genetic memory of his Father, the Creator.

Video in Russian

Music and performance: the Israeli composer Daniel Kruglov.

It is a well-known phenomenon that one and the same being has several different names in Scripture. God Himself calls Himself by many names, each of which reveals His character.

In Hebrew a human being also has several names. A man, say, has four:

  • GEVER (גֶּבֶר): a man, strong and brave; this points to his function and responsibility.
  • ISH (אִישׁ): a person (that is, one who has character, will and feelings).
  • ADAM (BEN ADAM): the son of the one created by the Creator from the dust.
  • ZAKHAR (זָכָר), from the word "to remember": one who carries in himself a genetic memory of the Father, the Creator.
