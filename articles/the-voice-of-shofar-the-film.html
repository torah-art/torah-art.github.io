The Voice of Shofar. The Film

2018 · Biblical Feasts

Do you want to know more about shofar? Watch the film "Voice of Shofar". This film reveals the mystery of the ancient Jewish musical instrument shofar, its symbolic and spiritual meaning, that is lost in the translations of the Bible from original Hebrew text to the other languages.

This film tells about the symbolic meaning of the shofar, the Torah giving on Mount Sinai (Pentecost), and about the calling of Israel
