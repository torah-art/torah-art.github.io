Biblical Feasts

Hearing the Voice of the Shofar: the Film "Shofar Experience"

2015 · Biblical Feasts

"All ye inhabitants of the world, and ye dwellers on the earth, when an ensign is lifted up on the mountains, see ye; and when the shofar is blown, hear ye." (Isa. 18:3)

"Happy is the people that know the joyful shout; they walk, O LORD, in the light of Thy countenance." (Ps. 89:15)

Shofar Experience, clip 1

ABOUT THE FILM

Shofar blowing, musical improvisation and traditional Jewish prayer singing (in Hebrew and Russian, with English subtitles), live sound, 51 minutes.
This experience is the fulfilment of God's commandment to blow the shofar and to listen to its voice.
