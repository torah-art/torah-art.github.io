2016 · Art Midrash

Pantomime Improvisation | Bezalel (Ex.31: 2-11)

This pantomime is an improvisation performed by Swiss mime Stefan Rotman on the music, he was listening for the first time. The music itself was also an improvisation by Israeli composer Daniel Kruglov. And all this performance is about Bezalel - the first Israeli Artist who was called by God to create the Tabernacle in the desert (Ex.31: 2-11).
