Biblical Feasts

The Jewish Attitude to the Feasts of the LORD

2015 · Biblical Feasts

The whole Jewish calendar, and the Most High's attitude to it, are arranged so that a believing Jew lives from Feast to Feast. I compare it to the meetings of lovers (in Scripture the relationship between God and His people is often compared to that of lovers). These lovers still cannot be together all the time, but the Bridegroom promises His beloved that He will wait for her at an appointed time, at an appointed place (in Hebrew this is called MO'ADIM. When the Lord says "these are My feasts", He says "ELEH HEM MO'ADAI").

"…Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, Concerning the feasts of the LORD, which ye shall proclaim to be holy convocations, even these are my feasts."

Lev. 23:2

מִקְרָאֵי קֹדֶשׁ אֵלֶּה הֵם מוֹעֲדָי׃
