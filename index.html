Called to Create

The first person Scripture describes as filled with the Spirit of God was an artist, Bezalel (Ex 31:2–3). Torah-Art explores what the Bible says about creative work and creative people: art midrashim, the biblical feasts, Hebrew words, artifacts, galleries and films by Ephraim Kalish.

The author

Ephraim Kalish is an artist, illustrator, book designer and filmmaker based in Haifa, Israel. He has studied Bezalel for more than thirty years and is the creator of Torah-Art, a project of art and film on biblical themes.
