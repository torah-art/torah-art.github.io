Books

For thirty years Ephraim Kalish has studied the figure of Bezalel and the theme of creativity in the Bible. The book series "An Artist from God" gathers this work into books for artists and for all who think creatively.

About the series

  • Ephraim Kalish: Books on Creativity in the Bible2023 · Books, The Bible and the Artist

Bezalel. The Mystery of the Artist's Lineage

The Bible tells us almost nothing about Bezalel's life, yet it repeats his genealogy three times. This illustrated book reads those four names in the original Hebrew.

The book's website

Rediscovering the God Called Artist, Biblical Foundations for Creativity

A fresh perspective on the biblical roots of artistic expression, centered on Yuval, the first musician named in Genesis. Digital edition, PDF and EPUB.
